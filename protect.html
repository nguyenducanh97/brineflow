BrineFlow — lock the page

Encrypts the tool with an ID and password. Everything happens in this browser — nothing is uploaded, and the password is never stored anywhere.

Both the ID and the password are needed to open the result — they are combined and stretched into an AES-256 key with PBKDF2 (310,000 rounds, SHA-256). Neither is written into the file, so there is nothing in it to read off.

  1. Keep your unencrypted copy somewhere safe — the download replaces it.
  2. Put the downloaded index.html in the repo folder, commit, push.
  3. Anyone opening the site gets a lock screen until they enter the ID and password.
